SSC CGL Dictionary Order Reasoning Practice

Dictionary Order tests your ability to quickly and accurately sort words exactly as they would appear in a standard English dictionary. It requires strict attention to alphabetical sequence.

How to Approach This Topic

Compare the words letter by letter from left to right. When the first few letters are identical (common prefixes), look at the first differing character to determine the correct order. Do not skip letters or rush the comparison.

Common Question Patterns

Questions typically ask you to arrange 5 or 6 words in alphabetical order and either identify the correct overall sequence from the options, or pinpoint which word appears at a specific position (like the third or fifth word).
