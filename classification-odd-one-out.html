SSC CGL Classification (Odd One Out) Practice

What this topic tests

Evaluates your ability to group items based on common characteristics and identify the single outlier that does not share that logic.

How to Approach This Topic

Find the common rule that binds three out of the four options together. The correct answer is the one that violates this specific rule.

Common Question Patterns

Number pairs (e.g., squares, multiples), word categories (e.g., capitals, synonyms), and letter groups (e.g., vowel presence, positional shifts).

Common Mistakes

Choosing an option because it looks unique in one specific way, without first establishing the concrete rule that unites the other three.
